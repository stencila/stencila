# TEI fixtures

Fixtures for `stencila-codec-tei`. Each `*.tei.xml` is snapshotted by
`tests/examples.rs` (decoded JSON, losses, and a structure summary), and each
GROBID fixture with a `*.jats.xml` counterpart is compared with the JATS decode
of the same article (`compare_with_jats`).

## Generated with GROBID

Generated by GROBID 0.9.1 (`grobid/grobid:0.9.1-crf`, pinned by digest in the
`Makefile`) from the publishers' PDFs, using `processFulltextDocument` with
`consolidateHeader=0`, `consolidateCitations=0`, `consolidateFunders=0`, and
`teiCoordinates` for figures and formulas. Without consolidation the output
depends only on the PDF and the GROBID version. The `-crf` image uses GROBID's
CRF models only; the `-full` image adds deep learning models for some fields
but writes TEI of the same shape. To regenerate:

```sh
make grobid-start
make -B generated
make grobid-stop
```

Press articles, from the PMC Open Access bucket, which holds the publisher's PDF
and JATS of the same version. The PDFs are downloaded to `pdfs/`, which is not
committed.

## Comparison with JATS

The snapshots `tests/snapshots/examples__<name>.comparison.snap` list, for the
TEI and JATS decodes of each article, the section headings in one but not the
other, those in both (with their level in each), and counts of figures, tables,
math blocks, citations, references and authors. The test asserts that the
numbers of references are equal, the numbers of authors are within one, and that
at least 85% of the JATS section headings are in the TEI. At the time of writing:

| | eLife 94334 | PLOS CB 1011696 | MSB 11611909 | MSB 11535196 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JATS section headings in TEI | 23 of 26 | 11 of 11 | 33 of 36 | 0 of 1 |
| Figures (TEI, JATS) | 54, 19 | 6, 4 | 13, 12 | 3, 2 |
| Tables | 0, 2 | 1, 1 | 0, 1 | 0, 0 |
| Math blocks | 0, 0 | 23, 29 | 0, 0 | 0, 0 |
| Citations | 120, 136 | 89, 93 | 79, 82 | 0, 0 |
| References | 68, 68 | 60, 60 | 46, 46 | 0, 0 |
| Authors | 7, 7 | 2, 2 | 7, 6 | 13, 13 |

The differences come from GROBID's extraction from the PDF, not from decoding:

- **Heading levels.** GROBID does not nest sections. The decoder nests them by
  the `n` numbering of headings, which none of these four articles has, so all
  their TEI sections are at level 1 while the JATS nests subsections at level 2
  (`grobid-sample2` is numbered and is nested accordingly).
- **Extra headings.** GROBID takes some lines to be headings that are not: the
  "Eq 12" labels and a formula in PLOS CB, fragments such as ")." in eLife, the
  rows of the Reagents and tools table in MSB, and each CRediT role in PLOS CB's
  author contributions.
- **Missing headings.** Back matter that the JATS gives a heading but the PDF
  states as a run-in label (eLife's "Competing interests" and "Author
  contributions") is a section without a heading in the TEI. eLife's "Appendix
  1" and one MSB results subsection are not detected as headings.
- **Figures.** GROBID makes a figure of each eLife figure supplement, source data
  caption and "continued on next page" page, and of some captions that it
  separates from their figure (the MSB correction). Two PLOS CB "figures" are
  equation text.
- **Tables.** eLife's two tables and MSB's Reagents and tools table are not
  detected as tables; their text is in paragraphs.
- **Math.** GROBID merges some consecutive equations into one formula (PLOS CB's
  equations 1 to 3 are one), and gives each as text, not TeX or MathML.
- **Citations.** A few in-text citations are not linked to a reference by GROBID
  (each is reported as a `ref[@type='bibr'] (unresolved)` loss), or are merged.
- **Authors.** GROBID takes "Al" (from "et al.") to be an author in MSB 11611909.
